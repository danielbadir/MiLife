The Idea

Lives & Moments

A Life is one person's archive — private, passphrase-protected, fully self-contained. A Moment is anything worth keeping: a conversation, a photograph, a voice note captured at 2am.

miLife does not sync to the cloud. It does not require an account. It does not know who you are. The archive lives on your device and nowhere else — unless you choose to export it.

What It Will Do

Built Around Privacy.
Built Around You.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every Life is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Your passphrase never leaves your device. We have no key, no backdoor, no recovery system.

Rich Moment Capture

Text entries, single and multi-photo moments, voice recordings. Everything you'd want to keep — in one place, private.

Chapters & People

Organise your archive by chapters (meaningful periods) and link moments to the people in them. Structure without complexity.

.life Archive Format

Your data is stored in a portable `.life` file — a self-contained encrypted archive you can export, backup, and move freely.

Truly Cross-Platform

The same `.life` archive opens on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS. One file format, four platforms, zero compromise.

Zero-Knowledge by Design

Offline-first, no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud. If you forget your passphrase, your archive cannot be recovered — by us or anyone else.
